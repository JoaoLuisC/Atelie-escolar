The commercialization of original teaching materials produced by basic education teachers largely takes place through informal and fragmented channels, such as social networks, messaging applications, and direct transfers, which results in the undervaluation of teaching work, the absence of transaction guarantees, and difficulty for other educators in discovering such content. In this scenario, this work aimed to develop Ateliê Escolar, a web-based e-commerce platform specialized in the promotion and secure commercialization of original teaching materials. The research, of an applied nature and qualitative approach, with exploratory-descriptive objectives, adopted bibliographic research and the development of a functional prototype as procedures, comprising the stages of requirements elicitation, architecture and database modeling, implementation, and testing. The solution was built with React on the front end, serverless Node.js functions on the back end, and Supabase (PostgreSQL) as the data and authentication layer, encompassing a product catalog, shopping cart, checkout integrated with a payment provider and automatic digital delivery, order management, a coupon system, an administrative dashboard with analytical indicators, role-based access control, and mechanisms aligned with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), such as self-service account deletion and audit logging of administrative actions. The results indicate that the platform centralizes and professionalizes the process of promoting and selling such content, reducing the informality and fragmentation of the channels currently used and providing a secure infrastructure for the monetization of teaching work.

Keywords: original teaching materials; e-commerce; web platform; teaching work; educational technology.
